# Design Context

## Target Audience
Homeowners in the East Brunswick, New Jersey area (and surrounding Central NJ) looking for professional tile installation, bathroom/kitchen remodeling, and custom stone work. Primarily residential clients planning renovations.

## Use Cases
- Learn about the company's services and experience (30+ years)
- Browse a portfolio/gallery of completed tile and remodeling projects
- Request a free consultation and quote via the contact form
- Find business hours and location

## Brand Personality / Tone
- **Artisanal & Crafted**: Family-owned business with decades of hands-on expertise — not a faceless contractor
- **Premium but Approachable**: High-quality work at a personal, local-business scale
- **Italian Heritage**: The name "Gaglio" carries Italian roots — warm, earthy, refined
- **Trustworthy**: 30+ years of experience, 500+ completed projects, quality guaranteed

## Design Direction
- **Tone**: Luxury/refined with artisanal warmth — like a master craftsman's showroom
- **Typography**: Italianno (script) for brand name, Cormorant Garamond (serif) for headings, DM Sans for body
- **Color**: Warm stone-tinted neutrals (OKLCH hue ~60), no pure black or white
- **Layout**: Editorial, clean, generous spacing — let the tile photography speak
- **Differentiation**: The script "Gaglio" wordmark paired with sharp rectangular CTAs and edge-to-edge imagery
